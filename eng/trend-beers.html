Website

Trend Beers

This project was originally developed in 2023 during my time as a designer at the Lado Norte agency. This conceptual redesign was carried out in 2026 on my own initiative to improve upon the original proposal based on my current perspective.

Role

UI Designer

Tools

  • Figma
  • Illustrator

Timeline

  • October 2023 [Original]
  • May 2026 [Redesign]

Description

The main objective was to conceptualize and design the user interface (UI) for a new e-commerce site dedicated to the import and sale of premium Belgian beers (brands such as Straffe Hendrik, Brugse Zot, and Blanche De Bruges).

The client was looking for a platform that balanced e-commerce functionality with a rich, immersive visual design.

Design Process

For this project, the goal was to optimize the information hierarchy and enhance the visual appeal to reflect the exclusivity of the beers. The key changes were:

Graphic references

For the moodboard, I looked for websites with unique features that resonated with the brewery’s theme. The main focus was on finding dynamic, rather than static, layouts that presented information in a functional way while incorporating distinctive visual elements.

Although the initial concept explored colors like red and yellow on a gray background, I ultimately decided to shift the aesthetic toward a dark, formal tone. This decision allows the design to complement the product photography of the beers, highlighting their quality and maintaining a consistent visual identity.

Navigation Map

Before focusing on the design, I created a navigation map to guide me through the pages that would make up the entire website.

Guia de estilo

See Design system in Figma

To ensure consistency and facilitate the scalability of the project, I developed a design system that includes a color palette optimized for dark environments, a clear typographic hierarchy, and modular components.
